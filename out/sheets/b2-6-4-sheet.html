③ 面积公式：S=12absin⁡C=12bcsin⁡A=12acsin⁡B=abc4RS = \dfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C = \dfrac{1}{2}bc\sin A = \dfrac{1}{2}ac\sin B = \dfrac{abc}{4R}S=21​absinC=21​bcsinA=21​acsinB=4Rabc​；
